## Fable4Sci skill

**Research → Structure → Fable**

Fable4Sci is an agent skill that transforms complex scientific and technical ideas into fables while preserving their underlying structure.

Instead of simply replacing scientific terms with metaphors, it first identifies the essential structure of a problem — entities, relationships, constraints, evidence, uncertainty, trade-offs, and failure modes — and then reconstructs that structure in a different story world.

The goal is simple:

> **Change the representation, preserve the reasoning.**

## What it does

Given a research question, method, result, or technical concept, Fable4Sci can generate:

* **Plain Fable** — a concise and accessible structural translation
* **Image-Rich Fable** — a more narrative and imaginative version
* **Fidelity Check** — a short check of whether the important scientific structure was preserved

It can be useful for scientific communication, teaching, conceptual exploration, and understanding familiar problems from a different perspective.

## Usage

Use [`SKILL.md`](./SKILL.md) as an instruction skill for your agent, then provide the scientific content you want to transform.
